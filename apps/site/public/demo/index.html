How to pick a backpack for a weekend in the mountains

Guide · 12 November 2026

Weight, volume and pockets: what really matters when you leave on Friday night and come back on Sunday.

The first mistake is buying it too big. For two nights in a mountain hut, 30–35 litres is enough: what doesn't fit usually wasn't needed.

The second is looking only at the empty weight. How the pack carries the load matters more: back panel, shoulder straps and hip belt.
